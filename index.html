FT8 needs your clock within about ±1 s of UTC. Best: sync your computer's clock (NTP). If the DT of most stations is far from 0, use "Correct from decodes".

Set the TX level so the ALC does not move. FT8 is constant-envelope; overdriving makes splatter that blocks other stations.
